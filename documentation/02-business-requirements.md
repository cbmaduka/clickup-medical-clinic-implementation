# Business Requirements

## Overview

This document outlines the business requirements identified during the planning phase of the CarePoint Medical Clinic ClickUp implementation.

The objective was to understand the clinic's operational challenges and configure ClickUp to support standardized workflows, improve task visibility, and enhance collaboration across clinical and administrative departments.

---

# Business Problem

CarePoint Medical Clinic required a centralized project management platform capable of coordinating daily operational activities across multiple departments.

The clinic relied on manual processes that made it difficult to monitor work, assign responsibilities, and track operational progress.

As the organization expanded, these limitations affected efficiency, communication, and accountability.

---

# Project Objectives

The implementation was designed to achieve the following objectives:

- Centralize operational task management
- Standardize departmental workflows
- Improve visibility of work across teams
- Assign responsibilities to specific staff members
- Monitor task progress through defined workflow stages
- Improve accountability using due dates and priorities
- Support collaboration between departments
- Create a scalable workspace capable of supporting future growth

---

# Functional Requirements

The ClickUp implementation was required to support the following business functions:

## Patient Management

- Register new patients
- Schedule appointments
- Track follow-up care
- Manage patient record review activities

---

## Laboratory Services

- Track laboratory requests
- Monitor sample collection
- Manage specimen processing
- Review and release laboratory results

---

## Pharmacy

- Manage medication dispensing
- Review prescriptions
- Monitor medication inventory

---

## Billing & Insurance

- Generate patient invoices
- Process insurance claims
- Track outstanding payments

---

## Quality & Compliance

- Record operational incidents
- Conduct internal audits
- Monitor staff compliance activities

---

# Operational Requirements

The solution was required to provide:

- Department-based organization
- Workflow-specific Lists
- Custom workflow statuses
- Task assignments
- Due dates
- Task priorities
- Subtasks
- Calendar-based scheduling
- Timeline planning
- Operational reporting

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

The solution was expected to be:

- Easy to use
- Scalable
- Well organized
- Easy to maintain
- Suitable for small and medium-sized healthcare organizations
- Configured using ClickUp Free Plan features where possible

---

# Success Criteria

The implementation would be considered successful if it could:

- Organize operational work by department
- Standardize business processes
- Improve task visibility
- Support staff collaboration
- Demonstrate realistic healthcare workflows
- Serve as a professional implementation portfolio project

---

# Assumptions

The following assumptions were made during the implementation:

- CarePoint Medical Clinic is a fictional outpatient healthcare organization.
- Staff members collaborate through ClickUp.
- Clinical records remain within a dedicated Electronic Health Record (EHR) system, while ClickUp manages operational workflows.
- The implementation focuses on operations management rather than clinical documentation.

---

# Constraints

The implementation was completed using the ClickUp Free Plan.

Some advanced functionality, including certain reporting and dashboard capabilities, may require paid ClickUp plans and is documented where applicable.

---

# Conclusion

The identified business requirements provided the foundation for designing a structured ClickUp workspace that supports patient services, operational coordination, departmental collaboration, and workflow standardization.

These requirements guided every configuration decision throughout the implementation and ensured that the resulting solution reflected realistic healthcare operations rather than a generic task management workspace.
