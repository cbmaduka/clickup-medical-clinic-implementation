# Pharmacy Management

## Overview

This document describes the Pharmacy Management workflows implemented within the CarePoint Medical Clinic ClickUp workspace.

The Pharmacy folder was designed to support medication-related operations, from prescription review through medication dispensing and inventory management.

The implementation separates pharmacy activities into dedicated workflows, allowing staff to manage prescriptions efficiently, monitor medication inventory, and improve coordination between pharmacists and clinicians.

---

# Objective

The Pharmacy Management implementation was designed to:

- Standardize pharmacy operations
- Improve prescription processing
- Coordinate medication dispensing
- Monitor inventory levels
- Improve accountability through task assignments
- Increase visibility into pharmacy workflows

---

# Folder Structure

The Pharmacy folder contains three operational Lists.

```text
Pharmacy
│
├── Medication Dispensing
├── Prescription Review
└── Inventory Management
```

Each List represents a major pharmacy workflow.

---

# Medication Dispensing

## Purpose

The Medication Dispensing workflow manages prescriptions from receipt through medication preparation and final dispensing to the patient.

### Workflow

```text
Prescription Received
        ↓
Medication Prepared
        ↓
Ready for Pickup
        ↓
Dispensed
```

### Typical Tasks

- Receive Electronic Prescription
- Prepare Hypertension Medication
- Notify Patient Medication is Ready
- Dispense Medication to Patient

---

# Prescription Review

## Purpose

The Prescription Review workflow ensures prescriptions are clinically appropriate before medication is dispensed.

### Workflow

```text
Prescription Submitted
        ↓
Clinical Review
        ↓
Approved
        ↓
Completed
```

### Typical Tasks

- Review New Antibiotic Prescription
- Check Drug Interaction
- Approve Medication Plan
- Notify Prescribing Physician

---

# Inventory Management

## Purpose

The Inventory Management workflow monitors medication stock levels and supports timely replenishment of pharmacy inventory.

### Workflow

```text
Stock Received
        ↓
Stock Verified
        ↓
Available
        ↓
Low Stock Alert
```

### Typical Tasks

- Receive New Insulin Shipment
- Verify Controlled Medication Count
- Update Pharmacy Inventory
- Reorder Emergency Medications
