# Billing and Insurance

## Overview

This document describes the Billing and Insurance workflows implemented within the CarePoint Medical Clinic ClickUp workspace.

The Billing and Insurance folder was designed to support the clinic's financial operations by organizing patient billing, insurance claim processing, and outstanding payment management into structured operational workflows.

The implementation improves financial visibility, standardizes administrative processes, and promotes accountability across billing activities.

---

# Objective

The Billing and Insurance implementation was designed to:

- Standardize financial workflows
- Improve billing efficiency
- Track insurance claims from preparation to approval
- Monitor outstanding patient balances
- Improve financial accountability
- Support operational reporting

---

# Folder Structure

The Billing and Insurance folder contains three operational Lists.

```text
Billing & Insurance
│
├── Patient Billing
├── Insurance Claims
└── Outstanding Payments
```

Each List represents a key financial process within the clinic.

---

# Patient Billing

## Purpose

The Patient Billing workflow manages patient charges from invoice generation through payment confirmation and billing completion.

### Workflow

```text
Charges Generated
        ↓
Invoice Created
        ↓
Payment Received
        ↓
Billing Complete
```

### Typical Tasks

- Generate Invoice for Consultation
- Send Invoice to Patient
- Record Card Payment
- Close Patient Billing Record

---

# Insurance Claims

## Purpose

The Insurance Claims workflow manages claim preparation, submission, insurer communication, and payment approval.

### Workflow

```text
Claim Prepared
        ↓
Claim Submitted
        ↓
Under Review
        ↓
Claim Approved
```

### Typical Tasks

- Prepare Insurance Claim Package
- Submit Claim to Insurance Provider
- Respond to Insurer Query
- Receive Insurance Payment

---

# Outstanding Payments

## Purpose

The Outstanding Payments workflow tracks unpaid balances and supports timely follow-up with patients.

### Workflow

```text
Payment Due
        ↓
Reminder Sent
        ↓
Payment Overdue
        ↓
Resolved
```

### Typical Tasks

- Outstanding Balance for MRI Scan
- Send Payment Reminder Email
- Escalate Overdue Account
- Confirm Outstanding Balance Cleared
