# Patient Management

## Overview

This document describes the Patient Management workflows implemented within the CarePoint Medical Clinic ClickUp workspace.

The Patient Management folder was designed to support the complete patient journey from initial registration through appointment scheduling, follow-up care, and patient record review.

Rather than managing isolated tasks, the implementation organizes work into structured operational workflows that improve coordination, accountability, and patient service delivery.

---

# Objective

The Patient Management solution was designed to:

- Standardize patient registration
- Coordinate appointment scheduling
- Track patient follow-up activities
- Improve visibility across patient-facing operations
- Assign responsibilities to staff members
- Monitor progress through standardized workflows

---

# Folder Structure

The Patient Management folder contains four operational Lists.

```text
Patient Management
│
├── Patient Registration
├── Appointments
├── Follow-up Care
└── Patient Records Review
```

Each List represents a distinct operational process within the clinic.

---

# Patient Registration

## Purpose

The Patient Registration workflow manages new patient onboarding and demographic data collection before clinical services begin.

### Workflow

```text
New Registration
        ↓
Demographics Collected
        ↓
Insurance Verified
        ↓
Registration Complete
```

### Key Configuration

- Custom workflow statuses
- Patient information custom fields
- Assigned staff
- Due dates
- Priorities
- Subtasks

### Typical Tasks

- Register New Walk-in Patient
- Verify Insurance Eligibility
- Capture Patient Demographics
- Generate Patient ID

---

# Appointments

## Purpose

The Appointments workflow manages patient scheduling and consultation coordination.

### Workflow

```text
Appointment Requested
        ↓
Appointment Confirmed
        ↓
Patient Checked In
        ↓
Consultation Completed
```

### Typical Tasks

- Schedule Annual Physical Examination
- Confirm Specialist Consultation
- Check In Returning Patient
- Complete Cardiology Consultation

---

# Follow-up Care

## Purpose

The Follow-up Care workflow supports continuity of care after patient consultations or treatment.

### Workflow

```text
Follow-up Scheduled
        ↓
Patient Contacted
        ↓
Follow-up In Progress
        ↓
Follow-up Completed
```

### Typical Tasks

- Call Patient After Surgery
- Review Medication Compliance
- Schedule 30-Day Follow-up
- Close Post-Treatment Follow-up

---

# Patient Records Review

## Purpose

This List supports administrative review of patient information to ensure records remain accurate, complete, and up to date.

Typical activities include:

- Reviewing demographic information
- Updating patient contact details
- Verifying insurance information
- Maintaining accurate operational records
